When we talk about a ‘‘hypergeometric deformation’’, we refer to every possible (first-order or higher-order) partial derivative of a generalized hypergeometric series Fqp​(a1,…,apb1,…,bq|t){}_{p}F_{q}\left(\!\begin{smallmatrix}a_{1},\dots,a_{p}\\ b_{1},\dots,b_{q}\end{smallmatrix}\!\middle|t\right) with respect to its hypergeometric parameters a1,…,apa_{1},\dots,a_{p} and b1,…,bqb_{1},\dots,b_{q}. These hypergeometric deformations usually satisfy some inhomogeneous differential equations, which in turn, can be solved by a standard method called ‘‘variation of parameters’’. Such solutions lead to integral representations of certain hypergeometric deformations of our interest, as illustrated by the lemma below.
