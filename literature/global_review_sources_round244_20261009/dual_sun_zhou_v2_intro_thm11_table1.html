1. Introduction

The four known classical rational Ramanujan-type series (see [32, Chapter 14]) involving cubes of central binomial coefficients (2​kk):⁣=(2​k)!(k!)2\binom{2k}{k}\mathrel{\mathchoice{\vbox{\hbox{$\displaystyle:$}}}{\vbox{\hbox{$\textstyle:$}}}{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptstyle:$}}}{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle:$}}}{=}}\frac{(2k)!}{(k!)^{2}} are

(1.1) ∑k=0∞(2​kk)3​4​k+1(−64)k\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\binom{2k}{k}^{3}\frac{4k+1}{(-64)^{k}} =2π,\displaystyle=\frac{2}{\pi},
(1.2) ∑k=0∞(2​kk)3​6​k+1256k\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\binom{2k}{k}^{3}\frac{6k+1}{256^{k}} =4π,\displaystyle=\frac{4}{\pi},
(1.3) ∑k=0∞(2​kk)3​6​k+1(−512)k\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\binom{2k}{k}^{3}\frac{6k+1}{(-512)^{k}} =2​2π,\displaystyle=\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{\pi},
(1.4) ∑k=0∞(2​kk)3​42​k+54096k\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\binom{2k}{k}^{3}\frac{42k+5}{4096^{k}} =16π.\displaystyle=\frac{16}{\pi}.

Note that (1.1) was first obtained by Bauer [13], and (1.2)–(1.4) were discovered by Ramanujan [52]. Usually, one proves such identities using the theory of modular forms (see, for example, [43, 51, 59]). The identities (1.1)–(1.4) have the following variants involving second-order harmonic numbers 𝖧(2)k:⁣=∑0<n≤k1n2\mathsf{H}^{(2)}_{k}\mathrel{\mathchoice{\vbox{\hbox{$\displaystyle:$}}}{\vbox{\hbox{$\textstyle:$}}}{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptstyle:$}}}{\vbox{\hbox{$\scriptscriptstyle:$}}}{=}}\sum_{0<n\leq k}\frac{1}{n^{2}}:

(1.5) ∑k=0∞(2​kk)3​[𝖧2​k(2)−12​𝖧k(2)]​4​k+1(−64)k\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\binom{2k}{k}^{3}\left[\mathsf{H}_{2k}^{(2)}-\frac{1}{2}\mathsf{H}_{k}^{(2)}\right]\frac{4k+1}{(-64)^{k}} =−π12,\displaystyle=-\frac{\pi}{12},
(1.6) ∑k=0∞(2​kk)3​[𝖧2​k(2)−516​𝖧k(2)]​6​k+1256k\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\binom{2k}{k}^{3}\left[\mathsf{H}_{2k}^{(2)}-\frac{5}{16}\mathsf{H}_{k}^{(2)}\right]\frac{6k+1}{256^{k}} =π12,\displaystyle=\frac{\pi}{12},
(1.7) ∑k=0∞(2​kk)3​[𝖧2​k(2)−516​𝖧k(2)]​6​k+1(−512)k\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\binom{2k}{k}^{3}\left[\mathsf{H}_{2k}^{(2)}-\frac{5}{16}\mathsf{H}_{k}^{(2)}\right]\frac{6k+1}{(-512)^{k}} =−2​π48,\displaystyle=-\frac{\sqrt{2}\pi}{48},
(1.8) ∑k=0∞(2​kk)3​[𝖧2​k(2)−2592​𝖧k(2)]​42​k+54096k\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\binom{2k}{k}^{3}\left[\mathsf{H}_{2k}^{(2)}-\frac{25}{92}\mathsf{H}_{k}^{(2)}\right]\frac{42k+5}{4096^{k}} =2​π69.\displaystyle=\frac{2\pi}{69}.
