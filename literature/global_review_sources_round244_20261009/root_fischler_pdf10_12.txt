3 A non-explicit rational function
In this section we construct the rational function Fn(X) that will be used in §4 to prove
Theorems 1 and 2. The output of this construction is stated as Theorem 4 in §3.1. Its
proof, based on Siegel’s lemma, is given in §3.5. It relies on a result of [13], which relates
asymptotic estimates of Fn(X) at infinity to values at 1 of some functions Pk,1(z) related
to a differential system arising from polylogarithms. In §3.2 we define these functions
Pk,1(z), explain the setting and state as Proposition 2 a technical result used in the proof
of Theorem 4. We prove Proposition 2 in §3.4, after dealing with a lemma of analytic
number theory in §3.3.
