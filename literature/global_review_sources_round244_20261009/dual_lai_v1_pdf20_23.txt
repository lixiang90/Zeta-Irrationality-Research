Remark 6.7. One can show a better real estimate by shifting the contour and using the equilibrium
measure (see [43]). For the p-adic estimate in Proposition 5.5, by using more economical estimates in the
summation over p process and a more tedious case-by-case discussion, one can deduce from Lemma 4.4
a better arithmetic estimate. Therefore, the margin in the main theorem is slightly larger than what we
proved. Our approach is mainly intended to keep the proof as simple as possible.
