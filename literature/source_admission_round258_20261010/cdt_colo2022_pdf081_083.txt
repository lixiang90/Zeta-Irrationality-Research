2.3      Squares of isogenies
In this section we will describe a commutative square of isogenies of the form

                                                              `
                                                      E0           E1
                                                      q                q
                                                      F0           F1
                                                              `

We will focus on the case of isogenies of prime degrees ` 6= q. In particular, we are interested in the following
problem: given the triple (E0 , E1 , F0 ) together with the degrees ` and q, find the bottom right vertex of the
square, namely F1 .
   We will then try to generalize the process to the case

                                          `               `        `             `
                                    E0           E1           E2           ···       En
                                   q                                                   q
                                    F0           F1           F2           ···       Fn
                                          `               `        `             `

where we are given an `-isogeny chain at the top (E0 → . . . → En ) together with a q-isogeny E0 → F0 and
we want to find Fn (or the `-isogeny chain at the bottom).
   In this section we will take a modular approach based on the moduli interpretation of curves X0 (N). In
section 1.2.5, we observed that isomorphism classes of elliptic curves are parametrized by points on Y (1).
Further, the construction of modular polynomials, and the fact that they provide models for X0 (N) (see
Section 2.2.2), permits one to infer that points on Y0 (N) represent elliptic curves together with an N-isogeny.
We formalize the previous observations in the following Theorem.

Theorem 2.55. The points of Y0 (N) are in bijection with the set of isomorphism classes of couples (E, CN )
of an elliptic curve E together with a cyclic subgroup CN of order N. Since subgroups of Elliptic curves are
in bijection with isogenies, this is equivalent to saying that points Y0 (N) represent isomorphism classes of
couples (E, φN ) of an elliptic curve E together with an isogeny of degree N.

Proof. See [DS, Th. 1.5.1].
