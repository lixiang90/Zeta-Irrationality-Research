Combined with Lindemann’s theorem [Lin1882] on the transcendence of π and,
more generally, of the natural logarithms of algebraic numbers other than 0 or 1,
one knows all of these values to be transcendental. The remaining L-values are far

Wei1885] only a single further explicit number L(k, χ) has been shown to be irra-

Coh78]. In this paper, we establish the irrationality of a new L-value L(k, χ);
