66: **003. The quasi-Riemann hypothesis.** Proves that every Dirichlet <i>L</i>-function, including $`\zeta(s)`$, is zero-free in $`\Re s\gt 7/8`$, resolving the quasi-Riemann hypothesis. The same half-plane is zero-free for every finite-order Hecke <i>L</i>-function over $`\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-3})`$. A companion gives a different proof of the zero-free half-plane $`\Re s\gt 11/12`$. ([Lean](lean/docs/003.md))
125: **005. Irrationality of Catalan’s constant.** Proves that Catalan's constant $`G=\sum_{j\ge0}(-1)^j/(2j+1)^2`$ is irrational. ([Lean](lean/docs/005.md))
182: **008. The Deligne–Drinfeld conjecture.** Proves that the rational Grothendieck–Teichmüller Lie algebra, with the Ihara bracket, is freely generated by one element in each odd weight 3, 5, 7, …, resolving the Deligne–Drinfeld conjecture. ([Lean](lean/docs/008.md))
488: **017. The irrationality exponent of <i>π</i> is 2.** Proves that the irrationality exponent of <i>π</i> is exactly 2: for every <i>ε</i> &gt; 0 and all sufficiently large denominators <i>q</i>, every rational $`p/q`$ satisfies $`|\pi-p/q|\ge q^{-2-\varepsilon}`$. This also proves convergence of the Flint–Hills series $`\sum_{n\ge1}1/(n^3\sin^2 n)`$, with angles in radians. ([Lean](lean/docs/017.md))
