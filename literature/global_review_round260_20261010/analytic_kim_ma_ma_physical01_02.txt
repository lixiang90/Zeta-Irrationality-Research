    Abstract. We provide various estimates of the hyperbolic metric on the twice punctured plane
C\{0, 1} and apply them to improve Landau’s Theorem. We also improve Ahlfors’ upper bound
for the hyperbolic metric on the twice punctured plane C\{0, 1}.
