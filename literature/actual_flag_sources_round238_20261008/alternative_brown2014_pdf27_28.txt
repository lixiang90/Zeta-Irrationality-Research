Remark 8.5. In the case when the motive m(A, B) is self-dual, these observations give
some non-trivial constraints on the periods which can occur. For example, via the
equation S(ζ u (3, 5)) = ζ u (3, 5) + 5ζ u (3)ζ u (5), we see that ζ(3, 5) can never occur as a
period of a self-dual motive. Therefore the self-dual cellular values for M0,11 (which
we expect to be periods of self-dual motives) should evaluate to polynomials in single
zeta values only.

                 9. Appendix 1: A short compendium of integrals
   The literature which has grown out of Apéry’s irrationality proofs for ζ(2) and ζ(3),
and in particular, Beuker’s interpretation (found independently by Cordoba) using
elementary integrals [5], is vast. I have selected a very incomplete list of integrals with
various irrationality applications and reproduced them here in their original notations.
The integrals below are referred to in the main text, but there are many others that
could also have been included.
