finite moments; consecutive supports disjoint or touching at one point;
no atom at a touching endpoint; integrable nested products; compatible
common root in mixed type. The new note explicitly proves that the
regularized negative-cut measure and root x*dnu_h satisfy the elementary
measure conditions. It does not identify the complete actual constrained
row space with the theorem's free polynomial coefficient space.
