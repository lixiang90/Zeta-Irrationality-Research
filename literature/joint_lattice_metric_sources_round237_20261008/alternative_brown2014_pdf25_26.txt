8.3. Remarks on the Galois coaction. The ring of motivic periods carries an action
of the de Rham motivic Galois group GdR = Isom(ωdR , ωdR ). This is equivalent to a
coaction by O(U dR ), where U dR is the unipotent radical of GdR . General nonsense
provides an abstract formula for this coaction (see for example [8], equation (2.12).)
